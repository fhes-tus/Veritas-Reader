Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Vern TTS, on-device audio, privacy, and file formats.

🎧 How do I get ultra-realistic, natural voices in Vern TTS? +
Vern TTS utilizes your phone's native Android speech engine. To unlock studio-quality, natural human voices for free:
  1. Go to Android Settings > Accessibility > Text-to-Speech output.
  2. Select Speech Services by Google (or Samsung TTS) as Preferred Engine.
  3. Tap the ⚙️ Gear icon, select Install voice data, and download the High Quality (Neural) voice pack.
  4. Open Vern TTS and tap the speaker icon while reading to switch to your new voice!
🔒 Does Vern TTS send any data to external servers? +
Zero data is collected or transmitted. Text processing, document parsing, and voice synthesis happen 100% locally on your device hardware. There are no user accounts, no telemetry trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no ads.
📱 Can I listen with the screen off or while using other apps? +
Yes! Vern TTS features a full background Android Media Service with lockscreen notification bar controls. You can play, pause, and skip sentences while running other apps or when your phone is in your pocket with the screen off.
📄 What document formats are supported? Can it read scanned PDFs? +
Vern TTS natively extracts and reads PDF, EPUB, TXT, DOCX (Microsoft Word), and Markdown (.md) files. Standard PDFs with text layers extract instantly. Scanned photocopy PDFs without embedded text layers require OCR software before text can be read aloud.
👁 Why are there 4 Paper Tones, and how does AMOLED Black save battery? +
Clean White provides maximum daylight contrast; Warm Sepia filters out blue light to eliminate eye fatigue; Soft Dark reduces evening glare. AMOLED Black turns off OLED subpixels entirely (#000000), drastically reducing battery drain during long audio-reading sessions.
🛡 Why does Android display "Install from Unknown Sources" when installing the APK? +
Android displays this standard security notice whenever you install an application outside the Google Play Store. Vern TTS is verified, safe, and completely open source. Google Play Store review is currently underway, and the Play Store listing will be available shortly!
📚 Where are the 35+ Curated Classics from? +
They are timeless public domain masterpieces sourced from Project Gutenberg, complete with authentic published cover art, famous quotes, and clean chapter formatting. They download on-demand so they never waste space on your device.
🔄 Will updating to a new version erase my reading progress or notes? +
No. When you install an updated APK over your existing version, Android preserves your local SQLite database, bookmarks, flashcards, and reading position automatically.
