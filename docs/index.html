Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding voice setup, formats, and offline privacy.

How do I customize and preview voices in Vern TTS?
Voice customization, engine selection, and tuning are handled directly inside the app:
  1. While reading any book or document, tap the Speaker / Audio Settings icon on the bottom player bar.
  2. Open the Voice Engine & Voice Picker to choose between available engines (such as Vern Lite, Vern Studio, Google, or Samsung) and preview different vocal styles.
  3. Fine-tune your reading experience with real-time sliders for Speech Velocity (0.5x – 3.0x) and Vocal Pitch.
  4. Optional: You can also download additional high-definition neural voice packs from Android System Settings (Accessibility > Text-to-Speech), and Vern TTS will automatically recognize and list them in the in-app voice picker!
Does Vern TTS send any data or books to an external server?
Zero data is collected or transmitted. All text processing, document parsing, speech generation, and reading statistics occur strictly on your device hardware. There are no tracking SDKs, no accounts, and no analytics.
Can I listen with the screen off or while multitasking?
Yes. Vern TTS features a dedicated background Android Media Service. You can lock your phone, put it in your pocket, or navigate to other apps while enjoying continuous narration with lockscreen media controls.
What document formats are supported? Can it read scanned PDFs?
Vern TTS natively extracts and reads digital text layers from PDF, EPUB, TXT, DOCX, and Markdown. Scanned photocopy PDFs without an embedded digital text layer require OCR prior to reading aloud.
How does AMOLED Black save up to 40% battery life?
On OLED/AMOLED screens, pure black pixels (#000000) physically turn off completely, drawing 0% current. Reading long books in AMOLED Black dramatically reduces display power draw.
Why does Android display "Install from Unknown Sources"?
Android displays this standard security confirmation for any APK file downloaded outside the Google Play Store. Vern TTS is verified, clean, open source, and will soon be live on Google Play!
Will updating to a new APK erase my library or notes?
No. When you install an updated APK over your existing installation, Android preserves your SQLite database, bookmarks, flashcards, and reading progress automatically.
